# MeiSense

**மெய் + Sense**

### Robotics & Embodied Intelligence

MeiSense explores embodied systems capable of perceiving, understanding,
and interacting with the physical world.

**Embodiment → Sense → Perceive → Understand → Act**

---

**மெய் (Mei)** carries meanings of physical form, reality, and truth in Tamil.

MeiSense represents the idea of enabling physical embodiments to sense
and perceive reality, transforming perception into intelligent action.
